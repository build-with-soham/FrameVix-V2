About FrameVix

Mood, Motion & Story

FrameVix started as one filmmaker's attempt to make YouTube feel like a real cinema. Here's the story behind it.

Hi, I'm Soham Chavan — Filmmaker & Software Developer.

I created FrameVix to give my films a home that feels as cinematic as the stories themselves. Instead of getting lost among countless uploads, every project here has its own atmosphere, identity, and space to be experienced.

I'm currently pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering with a minor in AI & Data Science while building my skills in C++, JavaScript, React, frontend development, and modern software engineering.

Every film on FrameVix is written, directed, shot, and edited by me. I love creating psychological thrillers, suspense stories, dramas, and cinematic experiences that leave a lasting impression.

FrameVix is more than a streaming platform—it's where my passion for filmmaking meets my journey as a developer. My goal is to become an industry-ready Software Engineer while continuing to create meaningful stories and digital experiences.

Thank you for visiting FrameVix. This platform represents my journey, my creativity, my engineering mindset, and every story I'm yet to tell. Welcome to my world.

The Journey

How FrameVix Came Together

Early 2024

Picked up a camera, seriously

Started shooting study vlogs on weekends, learning editing and framing ..

2025

Released "PARALLEL"

Two sides of an engineering student's life, told in parallel — the version they show the world, and the version they actually live.

Later 2026

Started building FrameVix.com

Decided the films deserved a proper home instead of a channel feed — and started learning HTML, CSS and JS and more skills to build myself.

Now

Streaming platform, V2

Rebuilt from the ground up with real navigation, a movie-detail page, a watchlist, and a lot more polish.
